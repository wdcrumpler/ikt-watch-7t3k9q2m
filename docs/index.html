How alerts work

Each night (about 02:15 Irkutsk time) the monitor fetches the previous day's full schedule and flight statuses for every airport from AeroDataBox. For each airport it computes the number of scheduled flights and the share cancelled, then compares both with that airport's trailing median.

  • Volume drop: scheduled flights fall below of the usual number. This catches airlines quietly pulling flights from the schedule, which never shows up as a "cancellation".
  • Cancellation spike: at least cancellations, a cancellation rate at least points above normal, and well outside normal day-to-day noise.

ALERT means Irkutsk is anomalous and the control airports are not. WATCH means Irkutsk is anomalous but so is the region, which is more likely weather or an airspace closure. A weekly check-in notification confirms the monitor is still running.
